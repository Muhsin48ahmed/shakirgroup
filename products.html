How to order

From enquiry to delivery.

Retail customers, workshops and trade buyers order the same way — directly with our team.

  1. 01

    Choose

    Browse the range here, or use the tyre-size finder on the Shakir General site to match your vehicle.

  2. 02

    Ask for a quote

    Tap “Ask for a price” on any product, message us on WhatsApp, call 323 from Somalia, or email sales@shakirgroup.com.

  3. 03

    Pay

    Cash, bank transfer or mobile money. Wholesale and bulk pricing for trade accounts.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Delivered across Mogadishu and shipped to other regions of Somalia. Returns accepted within 14 days in original condition.
