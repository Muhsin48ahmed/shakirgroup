Our story

Warshadda Isbuunyada Shakir.

Shakir Foam opened in Mogadishu in 2003 as the third polyurethane foam plant in Somalia, started by first-generation Somali entrepreneurs. It marked twenty years in 2023.

Since then the plant has been upgraded with new machinery, and the factory now creates more than 500 jobs, directly and indirectly. It invests in research and development, strict quality control, staff training and waste reduction.

Demand for Shakir Foam now reaches Ethiopia, Kenya, Djibouti, Eritrea and South Sudan. At home, the factory sponsors conferences at Mogadishu universities.

“To give people the best start in life every day by giving them the best sleep.”
