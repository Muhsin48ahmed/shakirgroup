## Findings

### Name and identity
- **Shakir General Trading** is the trading name on Facebook and on the group site (S5, S6, S8). The group's Facebook page calls it "Shakir General Trading Co." (S7).
- The sub-site brands itself **Shakir General**, tagline "Automotive Excellence" (S1).
- The Facebook avatar and award backdrop use an **SG** monogram in blue and grey (S5, S9).
- It is one of the group's two main segments, with Shakir Foam Factory (S7).

### Founding year
- **1990**. The sub-site says "Founded in 1990, Shakir General started as a small automotive parts trading company in Mogadishu" (S2), "Trusted Since 1990" (S1).
- The group itself was founded in 1982 (S6).

### What it sells
From the sub-site's About page, product range section (S2):

| Line | Items listed by the company | Brands |
|---|---|---|
| Tyres | Passenger car, commercial vehicle, heavy-duty truck, 4×4 and SUV, agricultural | Own Shakir tyre line; partner brands Aeolus, MRF, Westlake |
| Lubricants | Engine oils (synthetic and mineral), transmission fluids, hydraulic oils, gear oils, coolants and antifreeze | Alpet; own Shakir lubricant line |
| Tubes and belts | Inner tubes in all sizes, timing belts, V-belts, serpentine belts, drive belts; "compatible with all major vehicle makes and models" | Own Shakir lines |
| Grease | Multi-purpose, high-temperature, water-resistant, food-grade, bearing grease; "for automotive, marine, and industrial applications" | Own Shakir lines |

- **Batteries.** The group site lists the division as "Automotive tires, batteries & lubricants" and includes "Battery supply & installation" and "Parts & accessories" (S6). The company's Facebook post of 1 November 2026 promotes **INCOE batteries**, in Somali, and says to order on **323** (S5).
- The sub-site's partner strip groups Aeolus, MRF and Westlake under "Tyres" and Alpet and Incoe under "Lubricants" (S1). The Facebook post shows Incoe is a battery brand (S5), so the page lists Incoe under batteries. See the questions below.
- Products named in the online shop (S4), with brand labels: Shakir Premium radial 205/55R16; Shakir Commercial truck 315/80R22.5; Shakir Sport motorcycle 120/70ZR17; Shakir Tubes 17-inch motorcycle tube; Shakir Belts V-belt A-section; Shakir Lubricants 5W-30 engine oil; Shakir Industrial hydraulic fluid ISO 46; Shakir Grease industrial grease; Shakir Automotive wheel-bearing grease.
  - The page uses only the **sizes** from these listings, for the scale drawings. Prices, discounts, stock counts and review counts in the shop look like sample data and are **not** used.
- The Facebook intro says it is "the sole agent and distributer of well-known brands from around the world" (S5). It names no brands, so the page doesn't claim "sole agent" for any brand.

### Who it serves
- "From single vehicle owners to workshops and fleet operators" (S2). "Whether you're buying one tyre or supplying a fleet" (S2, mission).
- Warehousing and delivery: "whether you're picking up in Mogadishu or having it delivered" (S2). S1 says "Quick and reliable shipping across Somalia", while S2 lists "Mogadishu" as delivery coverage. The page says only "collect in Mogadishu or ask about delivery".

### Location and contact
- **Address:** 2nd Street, Bakaro, Mogadishu (S5). The group head office is 3rd Floor, Shakir Building, 2nd Street, Bakaro Market, Hawl Wadaag (S7, and the existing group pages).
- **Phone and WhatsApp:** +252 61 111 1100 (S1, S3, S5).
- **Short code / IVR:** 323 (S1, S3; Facebook post "Kana soo dalbo 323", S5).
- **Email:** sgt@shakirgroup.com (S1, S3, S5); customercare@shakirgroup.com (S1, S3).
- **Branches:** none are listed for the trading company. The group's branch offices (Baidoa, Beledweyne, Doolow, Dhusamareb) and its list of 13 cities belong to the group (S6) and are not attributed to Shakir General Trading.
- **Opening hours:** the sources conflict, so the page leaves them off.
  - The sub-site says Mon–Fri 9:00–18:00, Sat 9:00–14:00, Sun closed (S1, S3), and "6 Days Open Per Week" (S2).
  - The group site says Sat–Thu 8:00–17:00, Friday closed (S6).

### Recognition
- **Best General Trading Company of the Year**, 7th Somali Business Awards (SOBA), Mogadishu. Reported by Capital FM on 8 September 2026, alongside Best Foam Factory of the Year for Shakir Foam (S8).
  - Quote from Abdiaziz Ali Ahmed, CEO of Shakir Group: "We are honored to receive these two national recognitions. They reflect the dedication of our teams, the trust of our customers and the support of our partners." (S8)
- **Best General Trading Company of the Year 2024**, East Africa Somali Awards, presented April 2025, from the trophy in the award photo (S9). The sub-site lists EASA awards in 2025 and 2026 (S2).

## Left off the page on purpose
- "Somalia's largest distributor" (S6) and "Trusted by 100,000+ customers" (S2) are self-reported and unverified.
- "ISO 9001 certified", "DOT Approved", "ECE Certified", "API Licensed" (S1, S2) are not verified. DOT, ECE and API marks belong to the manufacturer or product, not to a reseller.
- "Official partner" and "sole agent" status for any named brand (S2, S5) is unverified.
- "First company to bring Aeolus tyres to Somalia, 2010": this is on the group pages (`index.html`, `about.html`), but no source for it was found.
- Payment methods, minimum order quantities, quote turnaround, warranty and returns terms. The sub-site's /returns page returns 404.

## Questions for the company
1. **Opening hours.** Which hours are correct for the Bakaro showroom and for WhatsApp: Mon–Fri 9–18 with Sat 9–14, or Sat–Thu 8–17 with Friday closed?
2. **Incoe.** Does Shakir General Trading sell Incoe batteries only, or Incoe lubricants as well? The sub-site lists Incoe under lubricants.
3. **Batteries.** Which battery ranges and sizes are stocked, and is fitting or installation offered at the shop?
4. **Branches.** Does the trading company have its own branches or stockists outside Mogadishu? Which of the group's offices sell tyres and oils?
5. **Delivery.** Is delivery available across Somalia, or only within Mogadishu? Are there charges or lead times?
6. **Agency status.** For which brands, if any, is Shakir General Trading the sole or official agent in Somalia?
7. **Aeolus, 2010.** Is there a source or document for "first to bring Aeolus tyres to Somalia in 2010"?
8. **Certifications.** Does the company itself hold ISO 9001? If so, who issued it, and what is the certificate number and scope?
9. **Trade terms.** Are there wholesale price tiers, credit or payment terms for fleets and workshops, or minimum order quantities?
10. **Payment.** Which methods are accepted: cash, bank transfer, EVC Plus or other mobile money?
11. **Photos.** Can the company supply its own photos of the shop, warehouse and stock (tyres, oil drums, batteries)? These would replace the drawings.
12. **Shop data.** Are the prices, stock counts and ratings on general.shakirgroup.com/shop real, or placeholders?
13. **Parts.** Beyond tubes and belts, which parts and accessories are carried?
14. **Awards.** Should the page list SOBA wins for earlier years? The group site claims five Best General Trading Company wins (2021–2024 and 2026), but only the 7th-edition win is independently reported (S8).
