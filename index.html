The group

From a trading house in Bakaro Market to four companies in two countries.

Shakir Group runs two businesses that manufacture, one that distributes and one that sources from abroad — serving wholesalers, workshops, hotels, hospitals and households across Somalia.

Each is run on the principles that have held the group together since 1982: dealing honestly, operating in line with Islamic principles, and keeping stock on the ground so customers are not left waiting on imports.

Our businesses

Four companies, one standard.

From tyres and engine oil to mattresses and made-to-order furniture, every Shakir company sells products customers need to last — and to be in stock when they ask.

01/ 04 Mogadishu
01 — Distribution

Shakir General Trading

Tyres, lubricants and automotive parts.

Supplies drivers, workshops and fleet operators across Somalia with Shakir-branded lines alongside Aeolus, MRF and Westlake tyres and Alpet and Incoe lubricants. In 2010 it became the first company to bring Aeolus tyres to the Somali market.

  • Passenger & truck tyres
  • Engine & gear oils
  • Tubes & belts
  • Industrial grease
Browse tyres and parts sgt@shakirgroup.com
02 — Manufacturing

Shakir Foam

Mattresses and polyurethane foam, made in Mogadishu.

Twenty-five mattress models across six brands — Shakir, Sweet Dream, Anfac, Comfort, Five Star and Layali — plus pillows, cushions, sofa sets and custom-cut foam for trade. The factory supports more than 500 jobs, directly and indirectly.

  • Homes & hotels
  • Hospitals & schools
  • Furniture makers
  • Cut to size
Visit Shakir Foam Best Foam Factory, six years running
03 — Manufacturing

Shakir Furniture

Furniture for homes, offices and institutions.

Designs and builds furniture to order for residential, commercial and institutional clients, with interior consultation for larger fit-outs.

  • Institutional projects

04 — Sourcing

Shakir Oasis General Trading

The group's sourcing and logistics office in the UAE.

Buys from international manufacturers and manages shipping, customs and payment for goods bound for Somalia and East Africa, through Dubai's port, banking and shipping links.
