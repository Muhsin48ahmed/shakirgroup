Common questions

How do I track my order?

Contact customer care with your order number on 323 or at customercare@shakirgroup.com. We will give you a full status update within 24 hours.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery for products in their original condition. Contact customer care to start a return.

How do I make a warranty claim?

Email customercare@shakirgroup.com with your purchase details and a description of the issue, and our team will take it from there.

Do you supply wholesale and bulk orders?

Yes. Shakir General Trading and Shakir Foam both supply trade customers, from workshops and fleet operators to hotels, hospitals and furniture makers. Email sales@shakirgroup.com or message us on WhatsApp for wholesale pricing.

Can you source products from abroad for my business?

Our Dubai office, Shakir Oasis General Trading, handles international purchasing, shipping and customs coordination for goods bound for Somalia and East Africa. Contact the Dubai office to discuss your requirements.
