About Shakir Group

Built in Bakaro Market, since 1982.

A Somali group of companies in trading, manufacturing and distribution — headquartered in Mogadishu, with a sourcing office in Dubai.

Our story

One of the largest suppliers in the region, still rooted in Mogadishu.

Shakir Group was founded in Mogadishu in 1982 as a trading business. It has grown into a group with interests in automotive trading, foam manufacturing, furniture and international sourcing.

Its two largest companies, Shakir General Trading and Shakir Foam Factory, sell through showrooms and directly to trade customers across Somalia. Shakir Furniture builds to order for homes, offices and institutions, while Shakir Oasis General Trading in Dubai handles purchasing and logistics for the group.

Today the group is led by Chief Executive Officer Abdiaziz Ali Ahmed from the Shakir Building in Bakaro Market, with branches in four regional cities. Shakir Foam alone supports more than 500 jobs directly and indirectly, and the group sponsors educational conferences organised by universities in Mogadishu. Since 2021 its companies have won eleven Somali Business Awards.

Milestones

Four decades, in brief.

From a single trading business in Bakaro Market to four companies in two countries.

  1. 1982

    Shakir Group founded

    The group starts trading from Bakaro Market, Mogadishu's main commercial district.

  2. 1990

    Shakir General Trading established

    Begins as an automotive parts trader, bringing quality tyres and lubricants to Somalia.

  3. 2003

    Shakir Foam established

    Mattress and foam production begins in Mogadishu, growing from a modest factory into a modern one making mattresses, pillows, cushions and sofa sets.

  4. 2010

    Aeolus comes to Somalia

    Shakir General Trading becomes the first company to distribute Aeolus tyres in the Somali market.

  5. 2021

    First Somali Business Awards

    At the awards' first edition, Shakir wins both Best General Trading Company and Best Foam Factory of the Year — a double repeated in 2022.

  6. 2023

    Twenty years of Shakir Foam

    The factory marks its 20th anniversary, supporting more than 500 jobs directly and indirectly.

  7. 2026

    Regional and national recognition

    Shakir Foam is named Best Foam Factory at the East Africa Somali Awards in Nairobi; both companies win again at the Somali Business Awards; and the group is named among 5 Magazine's Top 25 Somali Brands.
