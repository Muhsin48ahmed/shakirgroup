Our story

From a small parts trader in Bakaro.

Shakir General Trading started in 1990 as a small automotive parts trading company in Mogadishu. Today it is one of the two main businesses of Shakir Group, alongside Shakir Foam Factory.

It was named Best General Trading Company of the Year at the 7th Somali Business Awards in Mogadishu, and at the East Africa Somali Awards in Nairobi.

“To give Somalia access to quality tyres, lubricants, and automotive parts, backed by a team that knows the products and can help you pick the right one.”

Our mission

“For us, these awards are not simply a celebration of what we have achieved; they are a responsibility to continue improving, investing and building businesses that create lasting value for Somalia.”

Abdiaziz Ali Ahmed, CEO, Shakir Group
